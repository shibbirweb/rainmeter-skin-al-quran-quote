# Frequently Asked Questions

### Does it cost anything?

No. Al-Quran Quote is free and open source, and the verse data comes from the free quran.com service.

### Does it need the internet?

No. It works completely offline using a built-in verse list. Internet is only used if you turn on **Online
fetch**, which lets it pull fresh random verses (and other languages) from quran.com.

### Which translation does it use?

By default, the **Saheeh International** English translation. When online fetch is on, you can switch to
many other languages and translations, or the original Arabic. See **[Languages](Languages)**.

### How do I change the verse right now?

Click the small **arrow** next to the reference, or use the **Change verse** button on the settings
Verse tab.

### How do I make it change verses more or less often?

On the **Verse** tab, set the **duration** (in seconds), or turn **automatic rotation** off entirely. See
**[Verses & Content](Verses-and-Content)**.

### Can I show my own verse or dua?

Yes. Turn on **Use custom verse** on the Verse tab and type your text. See
**[Offline & Custom Verses](Offline-and-Custom-Verses)**.

### Can I put a verse on each of my monitors?

Yes, up to 8 windows, each independent. See **[Multiple Windows](Multiple-Windows)**.

### Will I lose my settings when I install an update?

No. When you install a newer `.rmskin`, your personal settings are preserved.

### The panel is in the way. Can I move it?

Yes. Just click and drag it anywhere on the desktop.

### The settings icon disappeared. How do I get it back?

It was probably hidden. Open Rainmeter, go to **Manage**, and load **`AlQuranQuote\Settings`**. See
**[Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting)**.

### It shows boxes or question marks instead of letters.

The chosen font does not include that language's characters. Pick a font that supports the script on the
**Text** tab. See **[Languages](Languages)** and **[Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting)**.

### How do I completely remove it?

Right-click the panel and choose **Unload skin**, or delete the skin from Rainmeter's Manage window. See
**[Installation](Installation)**.
