# The Settings Panel

The settings panel is where you customise everything, without touching any files. Changes apply
immediately and are remembered between restarts.

## Opening and closing it

- **Open:** click the small **settings icon** (three sliders) on the verse panel.
- **Close:** click the **X** in the corner of the settings panel, or click the settings icon again.

The panel opens right next to the verse. You can drag it somewhere else if it is in the way.

## The three tabs

To keep things tidy, the settings are split into three tabs across the top:

### 1. Text

Everything about the words themselves:

- Font, size, style (Bold / Regular / Italic) and colour for the **quote**.
- The same set of controls for the **reference** line.
- A **drop shadow** for the quote, with its own colour and an adjustable offset.

Full details: **[Text & Appearance](Text-and-Appearance)**.

### 2. Panel

Everything about the box the text sits in:

- Background **colour** and **transparency**.
- Border transparency.
- **Width** and **height** (automatic or a fixed size).
- How many **windows** to show (one per monitor).
- Show or hide the settings icon and the next-verse arrow.

Full details: **[Panel & Size](Panel-and-Size)** and **[Multiple Windows](Multiple-Windows)**.

### 3. Verse

Everything about which verse is shown and when:

- Turn **automatic rotation** on or off, and set the **timer**.
- Fetch verses **online** or use the **offline** list.
- Choose a **language** for online verses.
- Edit the **reference label** and show/hide the label or verse number.
- Show your **own custom verse**.
- A **Change verse** button and a link to edit the offline verse list.

Full details: **[Verses & Content](Verses-and-Content)**.

## How the controls work

- **Sliders** - click anywhere on the bar to set a value, or hover and use your mouse **scroll wheel** to
  nudge it up or down.
- **Checkboxes** - click the box to turn something on or off.
- **Colour pickers** - a preview swatch plus sliders for Red, Green, Blue, and transparency (Alpha). You
  can also type the exact numbers.
- **Text boxes** - click, type your text, and press **Enter** to apply.
- **Lists** (fonts, languages) - click to open a list of choices, or type your own value.

## The Reset button

On the Verse tab there is a **Reset** button. It returns every setting to the original defaults. Use it if
you have experimented a lot and want a clean start. (Your custom text is cleared too, so copy it first if
you want to keep it.)
