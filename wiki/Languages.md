# Languages

When **online fetch** is turned on (see **[Verses & Content](Verses-and-Content)**), you can show verses
in a language other than English, including the original Arabic.

## Choosing a language

1. Open the settings panel and go to the **Verse** tab.
2. Make sure **Online fetch (quran.com)** is ticked. (The language only applies to online verses.)
3. Click the **Language** control to open a list of ready-made choices and pick one.
4. The next verse appears in that language.

## Something not in the list?

quran.com offers well over a hundred translations. If yours is not in the ready-made list, use the
**type a translation id** box and enter the number for your translation. You can find these ids on the
quran.com website. This lets you use any translation they offer.

## Arabic (the original text)

Arabic is a special option in the list. Instead of a translation, it shows the **original Uthmani Arabic**
of the verse.

## Important: choose a matching font

A language only displays correctly if the font you picked on the **Text** tab actually contains that
language's letters.

- **English and most European languages** - almost any font works. The default Calibri is fine.
- **Arabic** - choose an Arabic-capable font (for example *Traditional Arabic*, *Amiri*, or another
  Arabic font installed on your PC).
- **Other scripts** (Bengali, Urdu, etc.) - choose a font made for that script. The Bengali screenshots
  on this wiki use [Li Mayukh Unicode](https://lipighor.com/Mayukh.html), a free Bengali font.

![A verse shown in Bengali](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibbirweb/rainmeter-skin-al-quran-quote/master/previews/bangla-translation.png)

If you switch to a language and see boxes, question marks, or blank text, that is the font, not the skin.
Change the quote font on the **Text** tab to one that supports the script. See
**[Text & Appearance](Text-and-Appearance)** and **[Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting)**.
