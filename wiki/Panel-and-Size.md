# Panel & Size

These settings live on the **Panel** tab of the settings panel. They control the box the verse sits in:
its colour, transparency, and dimensions.

## Background colour and transparency

- Use the **background colour** sliders (Red / Green / Blue) and the preview swatch to pick a colour.
- Use the **opacity** slider to set how see-through the panel is. Lower means more transparent; higher
  means more solid. You can also type the exact numbers.

The default is a dark, mostly transparent panel that blends with most wallpapers.

> **Opacity vs. colour:** these are separate on purpose. You can change the colour without disturbing the
> transparency, and vice versa.

## Border transparency

The panel has a thin border. A separate **border opacity** slider controls how visible that border is.
Turn it right down for a borderless look, or up for a crisper edge.

## Width

Two choices:

- **Automatic** (the default) - a comfortable fixed width chosen for you.
- **Fixed** - untick "auto" to reveal a box where you type an exact width in pixels.

## Height

Two choices:

- **Automatic** (the default) - the panel grows or shrinks to fit the verse, so long verses are never cut
  off and short verses are not surrounded by empty space.
- **Fixed** - untick "auto" to reveal a box where you type an exact height in pixels.

For most people, **automatic height** is the best choice because verses vary in length.

## Show or hide the icons

Two checkboxes let you tidy up the panel:

- **Show settings icon** - hide the three-slider icon for a cleaner look. (If you hide it, see the note
  below on getting settings back.)
- **Show next-verse arrow** - hide the little arrow next to the reference. You can still change verses with
  the **Change verse** button on the Verse tab.

> **Hid the settings icon and need it back?** Open Rainmeter, go to **Manage**, and load
> **`AlQuranQuote\Settings`**. The settings panel reopens.

## Quote windows

The **Quote windows** control sets how many verse panels appear (one per monitor, up to 8). This has its
own page: **[Multiple Windows](Multiple-Windows)**.
