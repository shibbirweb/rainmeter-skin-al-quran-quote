# Verses & Content

These settings live on the **Verse** tab of the settings panel. They control which verse is shown, where
it comes from, and how often it changes.

## Automatic rotation (the timer)

- **Automatic rotation** checkbox - turn the timer on or off. When off, the verse only changes when you
  ask for it.
- **Duration** - type how many seconds to wait between verses. The default is **1800 seconds (30
  minutes)**. Common choices: 300 (5 minutes), 3600 (1 hour), 86400 (once a day).

Changing the timer never disturbs the verse currently on screen.

## Changing the verse yourself

- Click the **arrow (chevron)** next to the reference on the panel, **or**
- Click the **Change verse** button on this tab.

Both show the next verse immediately.

## Online fetch (internet) vs. offline list

The **Online fetch (quran.com)** checkbox decides where verses come from:

- **Off (the default)** - verses come from a list built into the skin. No internet is used at all. Great
  for privacy or if you are often offline.
- **On** - each new verse is fetched fresh from the free quran.com service over the internet, giving you
  the full range of verses.

> **Automatic fallback:** even with online fetch on, if the internet is unavailable at the moment a verse
> should change, the skin quietly shows one from the offline list instead. You are never left with a blank
> panel.

When online fetch is on, you can also choose a **language** - see **[Languages](Languages)**.

## The reference line

The line under the verse (for example *Al Quran 2:255*) is made of two parts you can control:

- **Reference label** - the text before the number, "Al Quran" by default. Click the box, type your own
  label, and press Enter.
- **Show reference label** checkbox - hide the label if you only want the number.
- **Show verse number** checkbox - hide the number if you only want the label.

These apply to both online and offline verses. If you hide both, the reference line disappears entirely.

## Custom verse (show your own text)

Want to keep one favourite verse or a dua on screen instead of random ones? Turn on **Use custom verse**:

- Type your text in the **custom text** box.
- Optionally type a **sura** (chapter) number and a **verse** number for the reference. Both are optional;
  leave them blank and the reference just shows your label.

While a custom verse is on, the timer pauses and the next-verse arrow is hidden, so your chosen text is
never replaced. Turn the checkbox off to go back to normal rotating verses.

More detail and examples: **[Offline & Custom Verses](Offline-and-Custom-Verses)**.

## Editing the offline verse list

There is an **Open file in Notepad** control that opens the built-in verse list so you can add your own
favourites. See **[Offline & Custom Verses](Offline-and-Custom-Verses)** for the exact format.

## Reset

The **Reset** button returns every setting to its original default. Copy any custom text you want to keep
before using it.
