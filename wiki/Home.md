# Al-Quran Quote

Welcome! **Al-Quran Quote** is a small, elegant widget that sits on your Windows desktop and shows a
verse from the Holy Quran (Saheeh International English translation) together with its reference, for
example *Quran 2:255*.

It is quiet and out of the way. A new verse appears on a timer, or whenever you click the little arrow
next to the reference. Everything about it, the fonts, colours, size, how often it changes, and even the
language, can be changed from a friendly settings panel. No file editing needed.

## What it does, in a nutshell

- Shows a Quran verse with its reference on a soft, semi-transparent panel.
- Changes to a new verse automatically (every 30 minutes by default) or on demand.
- Works **offline**: if there is no internet, it shows a verse from a built-in list.
- Fully customisable through a settings panel: fonts, colours, size, shadow, and more.
- Can show **your own text** (a favourite verse or dua) instead of a random one.
- Supports **many languages** for the online verses, including the original Arabic.
- Can show **more than one window**, handy if you have several monitors.
