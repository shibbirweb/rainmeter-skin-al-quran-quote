# Getting Started

Once installed, Al-Quran Quote just works. Here is everything you can do straight away.

![A verse on the desktop](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibbirweb/rainmeter-skin-al-quran-quote/master/previews/standard.png)

## Reading the panel

The panel shows two things:

- **The verse** - the translation text, in the middle.
- **The reference** - a small line underneath, for example *Al Quran 2:255*.

The panel is semi-transparent by design, so it blends nicely with your wallpaper.

## Getting the next verse

There are three ways a new verse appears:

1. **Automatically** - by default it changes every **30 minutes**. You can make this faster, slower, or
   turn it off entirely.
2. **On demand** - click the small **arrow (chevron)** next to the reference to jump to the next verse
   right away.
3. **From settings** - the settings panel has a **Change verse** button that does the same thing (useful
   if you hide the arrow).

## Online or offline?

- **Offline (the default):** verses come from a list built into the skin. No internet is used.
- **Online:** the skin can instead fetch a fresh random verse from the internet each time.

You choose which in the settings panel. See **[Verses & Content](Verses-and-Content)** for details.

## Opening the settings panel

Look for the small **settings icon** (three little sliders) in the corner of the panel. **Click it** and
the settings window opens next to the verse. This is where you change fonts, colours, size, timing, the
language, and more.

![The settings panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibbirweb/rainmeter-skin-al-quran-quote/master/previews/settings.png)

Everything you change applies **immediately**, and your choices are remembered even after you restart
your computer.

> **Don't see the settings icon?** It can be hidden. To bring the settings panel back, open Rainmeter,
> go to **Manage**, and load **`AlQuranQuote\Settings`**. See **[Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting)**.

## A quick tour of what you can change

| You want to... | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Change the font, size, colour, or add a shadow | **[Text & Appearance](Text-and-Appearance)** |
| Change the background colour, transparency, or size | **[Panel & Size](Panel-and-Size)** |
| Change how often verses rotate, or the reference label | **[Verses & Content](Verses-and-Content)** |
| Show verses in another language or Arabic | **[Languages](Languages)** |
| Show your own verse or dua | **[Offline & Custom Verses](Offline-and-Custom-Verses)** |
| Put a verse on each monitor | **[Multiple Windows](Multiple-Windows)** |
